# Silverpine Hunt v2.0 / v2.1: art pass (2026-10-30)

v2 is an art pass on Silverpine Hunt v1 (`docs/silverpine_hunt_spec.md`). The gameplay layout is unchanged:
* spawns, tower corridor and anchors;
* den, crag and werewolf, skull gate;
* landmark positions, routes and the mine tunnel.

The owner's feedback on v1 was "slightly ugly … too noisy" `[USER]`, so he asked for all five proposed fixes. He let
me choose the details and supplied two references for inspiration:
* MountainRoad, a photo-colourmap mountain;
* alpine_desaturated, dark conifer masses on shaded snow.

v1 stays as released in `maps/silverpine_hunt_v1*`, generated by `scenes/silverpine_hunt/`. v2 lives in
`scenes/silverpine_hunt_v2/` and `maps/silverpine_hunt_v2*`.

**Status:**
* Reached: generated · exported · parser-validated (own reader + pyspades 1.4.2) · server-loaded (piqueserver 1.4.2 babel).
* Not reached: client-inspected · playtested.

## What the references taught `[RUN]` (measured with `ref_tools/ana.py`-style stats)
| metric | MountainRoad | alpine_desat | SH v1 | **SH v2** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| neighbour colour jump, mean (top-down) | 34 | 25 | 56 | **29** |
| top-down luma mean / % > 175 | 105 / 7.6 % | 77 / 7 % | 115 / 9.8 % | **97.5 / 1.4 %** |
| snow share (top-down) | – | – | 27 % | 33 % (but shaded, never white) |
| foliage share | – | – | 18 % | **27 %** |

Three lessons came out of this:
1. Both references bake light and shadow into their colours, which makes the slopes read as shapes.
2. Both have big calm value shapes: dark forest masses against open shaded snow.
3. Both use smooth natural slopes, not strata.

## Changes
| # | v1 problem | v2 fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "wedding-cake" mountain strata with snow lines | Sculpted ring, wider (starts 50–66 from the west/east edges, 54–68 from north/south). It rises through walkable 12-high foothills into a steep ridged wall: ridged noise gives crests and gullies. Five ridge fingers run in from the ring. The falls massif and the mine massif are smooth domes; the mine massif keeps its 7-high rim wall. Snow follows slope and altitude (steep faces show slate, gentle and high ground carries snow). |
| 2 | patchwork ground (snow / grass / mud blobs, brown rectangles) | **A continuous cover model.** A smooth snow weight per column comes from canopy shade, trampling (paths, yards, den, tower lane), a few big bare patches and slope. A ground colour per column (needles / moss under trees, slush / mud on paths, winter grass / earth in the open) is blended with it, so there are no hard material edges and the yards and den fade out softly. |
| 2b | flat colours + per-voxel noise | **Baked lighting** on every surface voxel: moonlight from high in the north-west (normal from blurred occupancy), plus ambient occlusion. Base jitter is lowered to ±1.5 %. Snow is capped at a max channel of 212, and floors are lifted to luma ≥ 40 (owner rule). |
| 3 | confetti trees (8 random palettes, orange flecks, speckled snow, even sprinkle, ~25 % snags) | **Groves and clearings.** A low-frequency grove field decides where forest stands. In a grove, canopies may touch; trunks stay ≥ 6 apart and the lowest branches are ≥ 4 up, so you can walk underneath. Clearings hold only an odd lone tree. Each grove takes its palette from a slow field over a calm family of 8 close conifer greens. Snow caps now cover whole tier sectors, not single voxels. Rust flecks, cones and frost speckle are gone, and Scots pines no longer get flat snow plates. Trees climb the foothills, each on a root plinth down to the slope. |
| 4 | plain lodge (long box, flat roof) | A main hall with a steep 45° roof and a gabled two-storey front wing facing the yard. The wing has a railed balcony off the upper floor and a big antler skull on its gable. Both have a 2-course stone base, and an outside stone chimney stands on the west gable with the hearth cut through the wall. The porch is on the wing; inside are open doorways, the stair to the upper floor and furniture. |
| 5 | spaghetti lake cracks, blobby pale skull | Lake: a smooth shallow-to-deep teal gradient, wind-swept snow dusting and five long meandering pressure cracks with side branches. Skull: a longer, lower cranium with a sagittal crest and a nasal bridge, darker weathered bone (stained toward the ground, moss at the base) and snow on top. |
| – | scatter noise | Random scatter cut to 650 tries with fewer bones, flowers and saplings; boulders and logs carry the scatter. |
| – | icicle fence along the cliff foot | removed (icicles only hang from the frozen brow of the falls) |

## Known limits / next
* Preview renders are numpy splats, not a client. Next: aos_web / BetterSpades screenshots to judge the baked shading,
  the snow share against the lilac fog, and grove density at play distance.
* The forest is denser (surface 566 k vs 514 k voxels; file 3.8 MB). Client FPS in the groves is untested.
* The walkable foothills now let players climb about 12 blocks above the basin at the map edge. That is far from the
  platform (> 110 voxels, beyond draw distance), but it is a new vantage over the back of each spawn compound, so
  watch it in a playtest.
* A faint vertical banding shows on the steep rock at the foot of the falls cliff in the preview. It is probably a
  lighting artefact of the column steps; check in a client.
